 Research Areas 
 
 
 
 My research focuses on experimental particle physics, currently focusing on hadron physics, especially systematic studies of hadron generation and decay mechanisms and symmetry breaking , as well as AI-based particle physics analysis systems . Currently participating in the Beijing Spectrometer III International Cooperation Group experimental project.
