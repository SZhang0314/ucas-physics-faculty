 Postdoc position 
 
 
 
 Postdoc positions on AI, funded by PIFI. The CAS President’s International Fellowship Initiative, PIFI, , which is applicable each year, is a specific funding program to attract talented foreigners to CAS for scientific exchanges and research cooperation. It is open to scientific research personnel from around the globe. PIFI offer 250,000 yuan per year plus round trip flight ticket subsidy to support postdoctoral fellows to conduct research at CAS-affiliated institutions for 2 years. Please submit an application with CV, sent to Yun-Song Piao electronically.
